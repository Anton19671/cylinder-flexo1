CYL_SOLVER postprocessor-only correction, 2026-08-27

Base: CYL_SOLVER_BC_AUDITED_STRICT_START_VISIBLE_2026-08-27.zip
Solver/STRICT MVI/BC/load mechanics were not changed.

Changes:
1. Automatic normal completion -> FAST.
2. User STOP with accepted steps -> FAST.
3. Partial/nonconverged termination with accepted steps -> FAST.
4. FULL remains manual (button); existing FAST->FULL queue is only used if FULL is explicitly requested while FAST is running.
5. FAST no longer calls _derive_step_and_save(): no reconstructed stresses/strains/sections/derived NPZ.
6. FAST no longer exports VTK or stress/strain 3-D fields.
7. Three displacement metrics are separated:
   - |w|max: global midsurface maximum;
   - w_center: diagnostic array-center w[Ns//2,Nx//2];
   - w_reference: fixed x=L/2, theta=0 control point.
8. CALCULATION_SUMMARY.json, RESULTS_INDEX.json and GRAPHS_MANIFEST.json record selected_state_file and selected_step.
9. Load_Deflection Excel explicitly exports and plots all three displacement metrics.
10. Final w(x) profile includes the circumferential generator containing the global |w|max and labels reference/index-center generators.

Important: accepted STEP_xxxx.npz / STEP_xxxx_STATE.npz remains authoritative. *_NOT_CONVERGED.npz is excluded.
