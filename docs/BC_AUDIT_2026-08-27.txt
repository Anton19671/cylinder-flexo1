Important findings and fixes
----------------------------
1. FIXED: CUF MVI ELASTIC_ENGINEERING residual-measure scaling.
   final_residual_scaling.py previously accepted only GEOMETRIC/NATURAL end rows.
   CUF MVI with ELASTIC boundary conditions therefore failed before the solve.
   ELASTIC residual is traction-like, so it is now measure-scaled identically to NATURAL.

2. FIXED: rigid-mode false convergence / non-unique displacement gauge.
   Previous check rejected only fully FREE-FREE. Partially natural pairs could still leave
   exact zero-energy rigid modes. Examples:
     KL S1-S1 / GUIDED-GUIDED: u=const axial rigid translation.
     KL S2-S2: v=const rigid rotation about cylinder axis.
     KL S4-S4: both u=const and v=const.
     CUF SIMPLY_SUPPORTED(S4)-SIMPLY_SUPPORTED(S4): u0=const and v0=const.
   These cases can produce a singular/near-singular operator or arbitrary absolute u/v.
   The BC equations themselves are physically meaningful; the problem needs a rigid-mode
   gauge. Until such a gauge is implemented, the solver now rejects these combinations
   with RIGID_MODE_HANDLING_REQUIRED instead of reporting misleading CONVERGED.

3. FIXED: MVI nonzero prescribed GEOMETRIC preflight.
   Current MVI essential-profile parameterization supports homogeneous qbar=0 only.
   FDM supports nonzero prescribed geometric data. MVI now rejects nonzero GEOMETRIC
   data explicitly at preflight with MVI_NONZERO_GEOMETRIC_BC_UNSUPPORTED and recommends
   FDM or a future lifting-field implementation.

Known limitation intentionally NOT hidden
-----------------------------------------
CUF SIMPLY_SUPPORTED is the physically movable S4 realization. Using S4 at both ends
leaves rigid in-plane gauge modes. The correct long-term solution is an explicit global
rigid-mode normalization (e.g. mean u0 / mean v0 constraints) that does not alter stresses.
This audit does NOT silently replace S4 by CLAMPED/S3 because that would change the physics.
Until that gauge is implemented, the pair is rejected explicitly.
