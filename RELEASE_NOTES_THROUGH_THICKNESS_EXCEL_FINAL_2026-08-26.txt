Scope
-----
Output/GUI/postprocessing only. Mechanics kernel and MVI formulas were not changed.

Fixed
-----
1. FULL now physically fills GRAPHS/THROUGH_THICKNESS for the last accepted step even when no control points are configured.
2. The public folder contains graphical z-profiles and NUMERIC/THROUGH_THICKNESS contains matching CSV data.
3. For CUF the profiles include u(z), v(z), w(z), E components, S components, epsilon_i, sigma_i and detF.
4. Birger-only GB and GB/G0 are published only when Birger is actually enabled. A service GB=G0 array in a non-Birger state no longer appears as a user Birger result.
5. Re-running FULL clears stale THROUGH_THICKNESS graphics and numeric CSV so results from an earlier run/step cannot remain mixed in.
6. GUI group 'По толщине' sees both public THROUGH_THICKNESS profiles and detailed XZ/SZ/Z_PROFILES sections.
7. CUF u0/v0/w0 selection automatically maps to physical u(z)/v(z)/w(z) when requesting a thickness profile.
8. Excel now contains the Russian sheet 'Описание_полей' with names, units and interpretation of field codes.
9. For Green-Lagrange, Excel explicitly identifies E as Green-Lagrange strain and S as the second Piola-Kirchhoff stress tensor.
10. 'Диагностика_полей' reports min/max/range and variation by theta/s, x and z, and identifies axisymmetric fields.
11. Birger columns in Thickness_Fields are explicitly marked '(только Birger)' and stay blank for non-Birger runs.
12. Surface maps display min/max/range and an axisymmetry note when circumferential variation is numerical zero.
